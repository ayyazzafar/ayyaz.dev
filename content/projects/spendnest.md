## About SpendNest

SpendNest is my personal expense tracking application. I built it because existing solutions didn't fit my workflow — I needed something simple but powerful enough to handle multiple accounts, shared household expenses, and detailed categorization.

## Key Features

- Multi-account support
- Transaction categorization
- Household expense management
- Clean, fast interface

## Why I Built It

Most expense trackers are either too simple or too bloated. SpendNest is exactly what I need — nothing more, nothing less. And since I control the code, I can add features as my needs evolve.
