## What is MyLife OS?

MyLife is an experiment in using AI as a personal life assistant. Instead of treating Claude as a tool I query, I treat it as a partner that knows my context — my projects, decisions, values, and goals.

## How It Works

- **Curated Knowledge**: Core files track who I am, what I value, and how I work
- **Journal**: Chronological record of conversations and decisions
- **Autonomous Updates**: Claude proactively logs insights and updates context
- **Portfolio Sync**: This very website (ayyaz.dev) is maintained by Claude as part of the system

## The Philosophy

After talking to an AI for a year, all that context usually disappears. MyLife is an attempt to make that knowledge persistent and useful — a true external brain.
